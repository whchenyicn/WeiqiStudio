One of the best things about Weiqi is that players of different strengths can still enjoy meaningful games together.

This is possible because of handicap games.

A handicap game gives the weaker player extra stones at the beginning.

These stones help balance the game.

They also create a better learning experience.

Instead of losing immediately to a much stronger player, the beginner gets a real chance to practice.

## What Is a Handicap Game?

A handicap game is a game where the weaker player starts with extra stones on the board.

These extra stones are called handicap stones.

They are usually black stones.

This means the weaker player normally plays Black.

The stronger player plays White and tries to overcome Black's starting advantage.

The goal is not to make the game easy.

The goal is to make the game balanced and useful.

If you are still learning the basic flow of the game, start with [How to Play Your First Game of Weiqi](/articles/play-your-first-game).

## Why Handicap Games Exist

Weiqi has a large skill range.

A strong player can defeat a complete beginner very easily in an even game.

That can be discouraging.

It can also make the game less educational.

If the beginner loses too quickly, they may not get to practice territory, weak groups, attacking, defending, or endgame decisions.

Handicap stones help solve this.

They give the beginner enough strength on the board to play a real game.

The stronger player still has to play carefully.

Both players can learn.

## How Handicap Stones Work

In a handicap game, Black begins with several stones already placed on the board.

The number of stones depends on the difference in strength.

A small difference may use two handicap stones.

A large difference may use six, seven, eight, or nine stones.

On many 19x19 boards, handicap stones are placed on star points.

Some rulesets or servers may allow different placement methods.

Beginners do not need to worry about the details yet.

The simple idea is:

The weaker player starts with extra stones.

## Why Black Usually Plays First

In normal even games, Black plays first.

Because Black moves first, White usually receives komi.

In handicap games, Black already has extra stones on the board.

That means Black has an even larger starting advantage.

For this reason, handicap games often adjust komi or use special handicap settings depending on the ruleset.

You do not need to memorize the details.

Most online servers and teachers will handle the setup automatically.

If rulesets feel confusing, read [Chinese Rules vs Japanese Rules in Weiqi](/articles/chinese-rules-vs-japanese-rules).

## Typical Handicap Levels

Handicap games often use two to nine stones.

Here is a beginner-friendly guide:

| Handicap | Typical Strength Difference | Beginner Recommendation |
|----------|-----------------------------|-------------------------|
| 2 stones | Slightly stronger opponent | Good for close teaching games |
| 3 stones | Noticeable difference | Useful when the stronger player wins most even games |
| 4 stones | Clear strength gap | Good for beginner vs improving player |
| 5 stones | Large difference | Helpful for learning against much stronger players |
| 6 stones | Very large difference | Use when even games are not educational |
| 7 stones | Very large difference | Good for teaching basic direction and defense |
| 8 stones | Extremely large difference | Use for complete beginners against strong players |
| 9 stones | Maximum common handicap | Useful when the goal is teaching, not competition |

These are not exact rules.

Different clubs, teachers, and servers may adjust handicaps differently.

The purpose is always the same:

Create a game where both players can think.

## Why Handicap Games Help Beginners Improve

Handicap games help beginners because they create more balanced positions.

With extra stones, beginners can practice:

- Connecting groups
- Defending territory
- Attacking weak stones
- Seeing the whole board
- Playing with confidence

In an even game against a strong player, a beginner may feel crushed before learning anything.

In a handicap game, the beginner has support on the board.

Mistakes still matter, but the game lasts long enough to be useful.

## Why Stronger Players Should Embrace Handicap Games

Handicap games are not only for the weaker player.

They also help stronger players.

The stronger player must be patient.

They must find efficient moves.

They must create complications without simply overpowering the beginner.

They must explain ideas clearly if the game is a teaching game.

This makes handicap games valuable for both sides.

A stronger player who can give a good handicap game is often a better teacher.

## When Should You Play Handicap Games?

Play handicap games when even games are too one-sided.

For example:

- One player wins nearly every even game.
- The beginner loses before reaching the endgame.
- The stronger player wants to teach.
- The weaker player wants a more realistic challenge.
- Both players want a closer game.

Handicap games are especially useful when a beginner plays with a friend, teacher, club member, or much stronger online opponent.

## Are Handicap Games Fair?

Yes.

Handicap games are designed to make games fairer.

Without handicap stones, a large skill gap can make the game one-sided.

With handicap stones, both players have meaningful decisions.

The weaker player gets a real chance.

The stronger player gets a real challenge.

Fair does not always mean both players start the same way.

Fair means both players can play a meaningful game.

## Common Misconceptions About Handicap Games

### Handicap games are not real games

This is false.

Handicap games are real Weiqi.

The stones, liberties, captures, territory, and strategy all still matter.

### Handicap stones mean the beginner is cheating

No.

Handicap stones are agreed before the game starts.

They are part of the game format.

### Strong players should never need handicap games

Even strong players may play handicap games when facing much stronger opponents.

Handicap is about making the game educational and balanced.

### Beginners should avoid handicap games

Beginners should not feel embarrassed.

Handicap games are one of the best ways to learn from stronger players.

### The goal is only to win with handicap

Winning is nice, but the real goal is learning.

Ask what the handicap stones helped you understand.

## How to Use Handicap Games Well

If you are the weaker player, do not waste your handicap stones.

Try to make them work together.

Do not start unnecessary fights everywhere.

Do not assume the game is already won.

If you are the stronger player, do not treat the handicap as a punishment.

Use the game to create useful lessons.

Afterward, review one or two key moments.

## Handicap Games and Learning Weiqi

Handicap games show an important truth:

Weiqi is not only about winning.

It is about making better decisions.

The handicap system gives beginners more room to make those decisions.

It also lets stronger players share the game without making the beginner feel helpless.

This is one reason Weiqi has lasted for so long.

Players of very different levels can still sit down and play.
