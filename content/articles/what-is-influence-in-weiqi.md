Influence is one of the first strategy ideas that feels mysterious to beginners.

Territory is easier to see.

You surround empty points.

You count them.

Influence is less direct.

It is the power your stones have across the board.

It may not score points immediately.

But it can help you build territory later, attack weak groups, or guide the direction of the game.

## What Does Influence Mean?

Influence means potential power.

A group of strong stones may affect nearby areas even if it does not surround territory yet.

Those stones can help you:

- Attack weak groups
- Build future territory
- Support your own groups
- Make your opponent's moves less comfortable
- Connect different parts of the board

Influence is not the same as points.

It is more like useful pressure.

It helps future moves become stronger.

## Influence Is Different From Territory

Territory is space you already control.

Influence is potential you may use later.

If territory is land you already own, influence is the ability to shape what happens nearby.

This is why influence can feel confusing.

You cannot always count it clearly.

You may not know yet whether it will become territory, an attack, or a helpful connection.

If territory still feels unclear, read [What Is Territory in Weiqi?](/articles/territory-in-weiqi) first.

## Territory vs Influence

Here is a beginner-friendly comparison:

| Territory | Influence |
|-----------|-----------|
| Immediate value: points you may count later | Immediate value: usually less clear |
| Long-term value: secure points if defended | Long-term value: future attacks, support, and expansion |
| Easy to count? Usually easier | Easy to count? Usually harder |
| Best for beginners: learn it first | Best for beginners: learn gradually through simple examples |

Both are important.

You do not need to choose one forever.

Good Weiqi often balances territory and influence.

## Why Beginners Chase Small Territory

Beginners often prefer territory because it looks real.

You can point to an area and say:

This is mine.

Influence is harder.

It may look like empty space.

It may feel like you are not getting enough points.

So beginners sometimes play many small moves to secure tiny areas.

This can be safe, but it can also become too slow.

If your opponent builds strong stones facing the center, they may later attack your weak groups or make a larger area.

Small territory is useful.

But if you only chase small territory, you may miss larger possibilities.

## Influence Is Usually a Long-Term Advantage

Influence often works over time.

At first, it may not look like much.

But later, it can make your moves easier.

For example, strong stones facing the center may help you attack an opponent's weak group.

They may help you connect.

They may make it harder for your opponent to invade.

Influence becomes valuable when the game moves toward it.

That is why timing matters.

If you build influence but never use it, it may not become valuable.

## How Influence Supports Attacks

Influence is often useful when attacking.

Imagine your opponent has a weak group near your strong stones.

Your strong stones make it harder for the opponent to run away.

They also make your attacking moves safer.

You do not need to capture the group immediately.

Sometimes attacking a weak group is useful because it lets you build territory while your opponent runs.

This is one reason strong groups matter.

If your own stones are weak, attacking may become dangerous.

If you need help with this idea, read [How to Save a Weak Group in Weiqi](/articles/how-to-save-a-weak-group-in-weiqi).

## Influence Works Best With Strong Groups

Influence is strongest when your stones are difficult to attack.

Strong groups can support nearby moves.

They can face the center.

They can help you pressure your opponent.

Weak groups are different.

If your stones are weak, they may need help instead of creating influence.

A weak group may look large, but if it can be attacked, it may become a burden.

This is why beginners should ask:

Are my stones strong enough to help future moves?

Or do they still need to be defended?

## Five Beginner Situations Where Influence Is Valuable

Influence can be useful in many simple situations.

Here are five to notice:

1. You have strong stones facing the center.
2. Your opponent has a weak group nearby.
3. You can build territory while attacking.
4. Your stones help connect different areas.
5. Your opponent is nervous about entering your area.

You do not need to calculate everything.

Just begin noticing when strong stones affect nearby play.

## When Should Beginners Choose Influence?

Beginners can choose influence when the position is simple enough to understand.

Influence may be a good choice when:

- Your stones are already strong.
- You are not leaving a weak group behind.
- The move faces a large open area.
- Your opponent has weak stones nearby.
- The move also helps your future territory.

Influence is risky when your own stones are weak.

It is also risky when you cannot explain what the influence is doing.

Before choosing influence, ask:

What will this help me do later?

If you cannot answer, a simpler territorial move may be better.

## Influence and Sente

Influence also connects to sente and gote.

A strong influential position may make your threats more serious.

If your opponent has weak stones near your influence, your moves may force responses.

But do not chase forcing moves just because you have influence.

The move still needs value.

If this idea feels new, read [What Are Sente and Gote in Weiqi?](/articles/what-are-sente-and-gote-in-weiqi).

## Common Beginner Mistakes With Influence

### Thinking influence is already territory

Influence is not guaranteed points.

Your opponent may reduce it or play around it.

### Building influence with weak stones

Weak stones do not create reliable influence.

Make your groups safe first.

### Never using influence

Influence should help you do something later.

If you build it and ignore it forever, it may lose value.

### Attacking too aggressively

Influence can support attacks, but beginners should not attack blindly.

Attack to build value, not only to capture stones.

### Ignoring secure territory

Influence is useful, but territory still wins the game.

Do not give up all clear points for vague future potential.

You can review more common habits in [Common Beginner Mistakes in Weiqi](/articles/common-beginner-mistakes-in-weiqi).

## A Simple Way to Think About Influence

Ask yourself:

Do these stones help my future moves?

If the answer is yes, they may have influence.

Then ask:

How can I use that influence?

Maybe you can attack.

Maybe you can build territory.

Maybe you can connect.

Maybe you can make your opponent uncomfortable.

This is enough for beginners.

You do not need advanced strategic theory.

## Key Takeaways

Before moving on, remember these ideas:

- Influence means potential power across the board.
- Territory is easier to count, while influence is harder to measure.
- Influence is usually a long-term advantage.
- Strong groups create better influence than weak groups.
- Influence can support attacks against weak stones.
- Beginners should not treat influence as guaranteed territory.
- Choose influence when you can explain what it will help you do later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is influence in Weiqi?

Influence is the potential power of stones across the board.

It can help you attack, build territory later, or support nearby groups.

### What is influence in Go?

Influence in Go means the same thing as influence in Weiqi.

It describes stones that affect nearby areas even if they do not make immediate territory.

### Is influence better than territory?

Not always.

Territory gives clearer points, while influence gives future possibilities.

Good play balances both.

### Should beginners focus on influence?

Beginners should learn territory first, then gradually notice influence through strong groups, attacks, and center-facing stones.

### How do I know if influence is useful?

Influence is useful if it helps you attack, connect, build territory, or make your opponent's position uncomfortable.
